      Matarix · Client

      A web-based ordering system with delivery tracking

      Client: Matarik Construction Supply · MNTSDEV · NU Fairview

      A group project for Matarik Construction Supply. The team is Maria Khelly C. David, Sherwin L. Gloriane, Henzo Timothy S. Quito, and Joshua A. Villaresis.

      The problem

      Orders come in by phone or as a walk-in, so the record is easy to get wrong. Deliveries are arranged by hand, with no shared view of where an order is. Customers wait without an update, and the order team and the delivery team can miss each other.

      What it does

      Customers browse materials, place an order, choose a delivery time, and follow it until it arrives. Staff keep inventory, schedules, drivers, and transaction records in one place. Payment is on site or by GCash QR, and an SMS notes the delivery scheduled for the day.

      What it covers

      Order entry, delivery scheduling, order history, accounts with roles, a status board, inventory, search, and feedback after delivery. A missed delivery is rescheduled by staff. The site does not show the driver on a map. Payment is on site or GCash QR.

      Why it matters

      The shop gets fewer order mistakes and a clearer delivery list. Customers can see the status of an order. Staff spend less time taking orders by phone. I built the website and interface, and kept the customer, order, and transaction records consistent.

      IncuVue · Client

      A smart incubator for a small farm

      Client: Organikong Filipino Farm · Flutter · Firebase · Roboflow · IoT

      A phone app and a website for one hatching batch. The camera photos are checked by Roboflow, and the website holds those egg results.

      The problem

      A hatch needs steady heat, humidity, turning, and a look at the eggs. Checking all of that by hand is easy to miss, and a problem can show up only after the batch has already drifted.

      What it does

      The app sets incubation and lockdown days, temperature and humidity ranges, how often the tray turns, and how often the camera takes a photo. The dashboard shows the live readings and alerts. Roboflow marks each egg in the photo as normal or cracked.

      What it covers

      One batch at a time: climate, water tray, heater and fans, tilt, and camera. The screens on this page walk through that setup. The website shows the egg results from those photos.

      Why it matters

      The farm can see the batch without standing at the box, and get an alert when temperature or humidity leaves the set range. I built the mobile app, prepared the guides, and added those alerts.

      AquaGrow

      A mobile smart plant watering system

      Programming Embedded Systems · National University · April 2025 · Submitted to Mr. Crimgene E. Villanueva

      AquaGrow is a group project for PEMBEDS. The team is Maria Khelly C. David, Sherwin L. Gloriane, Henzo Timothy S. Quito, and Joshua A. Villaresis. It is a small watering system for home plants, not a farm.

      The problem

      People forget to water plants, or they water too much. Busy schedules and not knowing how wet the soil is leads to unhealthy plants and wasted water. Reminders and self-watering pots do not look at the soil in the moment. Bigger smart systems are often costly.

      What it does

      A soil moisture sensor reads how wet the soil is. The system waters from that reading, so the plant gets water when it needs it. An Android app shows the moisture level and watering status, and the person can water by hand or set a schedule.

      What it covers

      Small indoor and outdoor plants at home. It watches soil moisture only, not temperature, humidity, or light. It connects by Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and the app in this project is for Android. It was tested on a few plant types and soils.

      Why it matters

      The plant gets water at the right time, and less water is wasted. It is meant to be simple enough for someone new to plants. It is also a first step toward sensor-based gardening at home.

      On this project I organized the plant and sensor records and tested the system against the results it was expected to give. There is no public website for AquaGrow. The app screens are on this page.

      Circle Coffee · Client

      Online ordering and table reservations

      Client: Circle Coffee · MySQL · SQL · Interface design

      A client site for Circle Coffee. Customers order from the menu and reserve a table. The records sit in MySQL.

      The problem

      A café that takes orders and reservations by hand can lose the thread between the menu, the booking, and what was actually requested. Staff then have to ask again, and the count of orders is hard to trust.

      What it does

      The site lets someone browse the menu, place an order, and reserve a table. SQL stores the menu and the reservation records. The ordering screen is where the customer builds the order.

      What it covers

      Menu items, orders, and reservations for one café. The clickable preview is public. Orders and bookings are not stored.

      Why it matters

      Orders and bookings stay in one set of records, so what was requested can be checked against what was stored. I wrote the SQL for the menu and reservations and worked on the ordering interface.

      Kuwiz

      A mobile quiz

      Mobile app · Interface design

      A class quiz app. Each round runs on a timer, and the score comes from the answers given in that round.

      The problem

      A quiz only works if the timer and the score stay honest. If the clock or the points drift, the result does not match what the player actually did.

      What it does

      The player answers questions while a timer runs. The score is counted from those answers. The screen shows the question, the time left, and the result.

      What it covers

      One quiz session at a time, with a timer and a score. It is a class app. There is no public store listing.

      Why it matters

      The timer result and the score can be checked against the outcome they should produce. I tested those results and worked on the interface.
